Privacy policy

BET backup is a personal tool used only by its owner.

  • Data accessed: only files that BET backup itself creates in the owner's Google Drive (scope drive.file). It does not access any other files, email, contacts or other Google user data.
  • Use: files are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload and are used only to store and restore the owner's backups.
  • Sharing: no data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to anyone.
  • Retention: backups stay in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
